# Critical Design: Discussion

Based on Chapter 4, “Criticism, function, and discipline,” from *Critical Design in Context* by Matt Malpass.

## Discussion Questions

1. **Think of an object you love.** What makes you enjoy it. Is it because how it works, how it looks, or how it makes you feel?

2. **Pick an object in this room.** What does its design suggest about who it was made for? *You can reference objects you have or see around you*

3. **Imagine making an everyday object awkward or strange or outside of convention.** What could that change help people notice about their habits and needs?

4. **Imagine your personal work starts an argument.** How would you decide whether that reaction means the work succeeded? *Think about some projects you've made in this class or others*

5. **Look at something you recently made.** What idea about beauty, usefulness, or success could you challenge in that work?

## 5-Minute Sketch: Make an Object Ask a Question

Redesign an everyday object to make people think about something they usually overlook.

- **1 minute:** Choose an object and an idea to question, such as convenience, waste, status, privacy, or beauty.
- **3 minutes:** Sketch your redesign. Focus on communicating the idea; a rough drawing is enough.
- **1 minute:** Give it a name and finish: “This object makes people think about…”

Afterward, let a classmate interpret the sketch before you explain your intention.
